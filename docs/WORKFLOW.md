Example workflow
This is a conceptual workflow description, not a technical specification of the proprietary production system.
1. The clinician opens their existing PACS/RIS or compatible reporting application.
2. The clinician starts RadRep dictation.
3. The clinician dictates the findings and relevant report details.
4. RadRep assists with structuring and formatting the dictated content.
5. The clinician reviews the draft against the images, notes, and clinical context.
6. The clinician edits the draft as needed.
7. The clinician transfers or finalizes the report through their approved workflow.
Human review
RadRep is a documentation aid. It must not be treated as an independent diagnostic authority. The responsible clinician must verify the content and approve the final report.
