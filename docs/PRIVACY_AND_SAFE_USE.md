Privacy and safe use
RadRep.ai is intended for professional radiology documentation workflows.
Do not publish sensitive information
Never place the following in this public repository or its GitHub Issues:
- Patient names, dates of birth, IDs, or contact details
- DICOM files, scans, screenshots, or identifiable medical images
- Clinical records or report text that can identify a patient
- API keys, passwords, access tokens, or private configuration
- Internal security details
Clinical review
AI-generated drafts can contain omissions, transcription errors, or formatting mistakes. A qualified clinician must verify the report against the source material and take responsibility for the final signed report.
Security questions
For product-specific privacy, security, or compliance questions, consult the official RadRep.ai website and contact the product team through its published channels. Do not infer certifications or compliance status from this repository.
